Montage

Montage is a personal desktop application for Windows. It is installed and runs only on its owner's own computer, and it is used by a single person: its owner.

Montage is not a website, not a web service and not a mobile app. There is no server, no user accounts and no public access. Nothing is hosted online except these information pages.

What it does

  • Edits short vertical videos (subtitles, titles, cuts, sound) on the owner's computer.
  • Lets the owner publish a finished video to his own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts, through the official APIs of these platforms, after he has connected each account himself.

Where it runs

Everything happens on the owner's desktop. The application opens a local page on the computer itself (an address starting with localhost) that cannot be reached from the Internet. The platforms send the sign-in response back to that local address when the owner connects an account.

Distribution

The application is not published, sold or distributed to the public. It has no other users.

Montage is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok, YouTube/Google, Meta, Facebook or Instagram.
